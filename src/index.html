Progression & tracking

Movement guidance and substitutions

Push-ups: maintain a straight trunk and stop 2–3 reps before failure. Pike: raise hips and lower head between hands. Cobra press-ups: keep hips on the floor and press chest up only as comfortable. Reverse snow angels and Y-T-W raises work the upper back but do not replace pulling resistance. Use a secure resistance band for rows if available.

For challenging variations (diamond, decline, pike), elevate hands or shorten the range as needed. For lower-body work, use a wall or railing for balance.

Safety and recovery

Strength sessions should feel about 7/10 effort, while Tue/Thu/Sun are easy. Avoid breath-holding and maximal exertion. Stop for chest pain, faintness, severe headache, or unusual breathlessness. If discomfort persists, consult a clinician.
