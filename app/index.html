Movement guidance and substitutions

Push-ups: straight trunk, stop 2–3 reps before failure. Pike: hips high, lower the head between the hands. Cobra press-ups: hips stay on the floor; press up only as far as is comfortable. No equipment is scheduled. Reverse snow angels, Y-T-W/W raises and prone swimmers train the upper back and posterior chain at low load; they are not a substitute for loaded pulling. If a sturdy table edge or a band ever becomes available, table/band rows are in the exercise library as a swap. For diamond, decline and pike variations, elevate the hands or shorten the range when needed. Use a wall or railing for balance on single-leg work.

Tap any exercise name for a full how-to, or its 🔊 button to have it read aloud. In a session, tap "How?" under the exercise name.

Safety and recovery

Strength sessions should feel about 7/10 effort; Tue, Thu and Sun are easy. Keep breathing on every rep — exhale on the effort, never hold your breath or strain. Stop for chest pain, faintness, severe headache, or unusual breathlessness. If discomfort persists, consult a clinician.

Volume & burnout

Timed burnout on the last set

Burnout: as many clean reps as you can in the time, stopping when technique breaks down — not to absolute failure. Keep breathing throughout. Applies to push, leg, hinge and upper-back exercises, not holds or the core finisher.
